# Storyscope editorial checklist

Use this after the structural map and before final delivery. Mark each item
`PASS`, `REPAIR`, `N/A`, or `BLOCKED`, with a short evidence note.

## Structure

- Is the anchor concrete and early enough?
- Does pressure make the piece move?
- Is agency visible, including what remains outside anyone's control?
- Does each major section add a fact, scene, judgment, or consequence?
- Does the chosen shape serve the material rather than a template?

## Evidence and reader trust

- Is every anecdote real, supplied, verified, or explicitly hypothetical?
- Are source fact, author observation, and editorial synthesis distinct?
- Are causal claims supported, or should they say “after,” “alongside,” or “we do
  not know”?
- Are named references real, relevant, and verified?
- Does the ending preserve a meaningful remainder instead of inventing closure?

## Revision quality

- Has explanatory moralizing been cut where the reader can infer the point?
- Are direct address, flashbacks, subplots, sensory details, and ambiguity earned?
- Has generic promotional language been removed without flattening voice?
- Does chronology remain clear where clarity is the point?
- Did the structural pass preserve continuity, terminology, privacy, citations, and
  any required build or rendering checks?

## Shapes

Choose one only when its reason is evident:

- case to consequence;
- question to complication;
- object to world;
- chronological investigation;
- recontextualized sequence;
- braided chapter through shared pressure.

A linear tutorial, restrained review, or simple ending can pass. Variation is a
means of serving material, not a requirement to look human.
